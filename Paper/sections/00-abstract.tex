\begin{abstract}
We reproduce the weighted-potential method of Zhao, Booth-Clibborn and
Schwahn and obtain a modest refinement of their diagonal Ramsey bound.
The complementary moment inequality reduces the set of feasible
retained fractions without changing the optimizer on that set.
Revised construction parameters and exact finite certificates
give $R(k,k)\le3.49135655^k$ for all sufficiently large $k$, improving
the previous base $3.4986238$. The accompanying Lean development records
the proof and certificate data.
\end{abstract}
