\section{Formalization and certificates}
\label{sec:formalization}

The development uses Lean~\cite{Lean} and Mathlib~\cite{Mathlib}
4.35.0, RamseyLean's definitions and foundational lemmas~\cite{RamseyLean,GNNW},
the proof code and data of~\cite{ZBS}, and Lu--Wang's source and
analytic checks~\cite{LW}, including interval arithmetic originating
in RamseyLean. The \href{\leanrepo/NOTICE.md}{attribution record}
identifies inherited revisions and licenses;
\href{\leanrepo/Scripts/README.md}{reproduction instructions} describe
the build, axiom audit and built-in \texttt{lake comparator} check
against a separate finite-graph specification. The comparison permits only
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
